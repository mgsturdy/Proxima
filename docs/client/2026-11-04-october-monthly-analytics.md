> **October was the first month since July with no social spike, and the underlying traffic edged up.** The site recorded **1,164 pageviews from 528 visitors, 38 per day**, against September's 33 per day once its Facebook weekend is removed. That is a 15% gain in the baseline and back in line with July. The audience got more serious: **pages per visitor rose to 2.2, the highest since the bot-era numbers washed out**, desktop returned to 72% of pageviews, and **/diagnostics had its best organic month on record (262 pageviews, 182 visitors)**, overtaking every other content page. **Google recovered slightly (110 → 120)**. Practitioner inquiries held at **10**. The weak spot is conversion: quiz starts fell to 9, and diagnostics signups came from only 6 people despite the jump in /diagnostics traffic.

### Key observations

- **/diagnostics nearly doubled (136 → 262 pageviews, 116 → 182 visitors) and took 23% of all traffic**, the largest share any inner page has had. It is also the top Google landing page after the homepage (21 Google arrivals, up from 13). Without any change to the page, people are increasingly arriving at Proxima to learn about testing.
- **But /diagnostics bounce rose to 36%** (28% in September), and signups from it fell (see Events). More people are landing directly on /diagnostics, often from search, and more of them leave without going further. The page is now the second front door, and it has no quiz link.
- **The homepage fell to 32% of pageviews** (46% in September), mostly because no social share drove one-page homepage visits. Homepage bounce improved slightly to 56%.
- **/quiz bounce dropped to 6%.** Almost everyone who reaches the quiz page starts interacting with it. The problem is getting people there, not keeping them.
- **The blog recorded zero pageviews.** Still unlisted, by design. Seven posts shipped in October (vinyl chloride, barium, selenium, antimony, cobalt, thallium, 1-bromopropane), bringing the unlisted library to roughly 77 posts.
- **Guessed URLs:** /careers, /contact, /tests (one each). /contact has now been tried three months running.
