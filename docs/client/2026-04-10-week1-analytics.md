## Recommendations

1. **Double down on content sharing** — 81% direct traffic suggests the link is being passed around. Provide easy share links and consider a "Share with a colleague" CTA.
2. **Monitor SEO growth** — 11 Google pageviews in 3 days is promising for a brand-new site with 25 blog posts. Track which keywords are driving traffic.
3. **Investigate Thailand traffic** — 7% from Thailand is unexpected. If it's organic, there may be a practitioner community to engage.
4. **Optimize the quiz email capture** — 50% email rate is good, but the 3 skips represent lost leads. Consider adding more value to the email prompt (e.g., "Get your personalized report via email").
5. **Mobile experience** — 24% mobile traffic is significant. Ensure the quiz and signup flows work smoothly on mobile.
6. **CTA optimization** — only 10 CTA clicks from 439 pageviews (2.3%). Test different copy, colors, or placement.
