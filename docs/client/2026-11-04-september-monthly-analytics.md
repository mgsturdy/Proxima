> **September was a return to baseline, with one Facebook weekend on top.** The site recorded **1,227 pageviews from 605 visitors**, back to July's level after August's X-driven record. The underlying run rate outside the spike was **about 33 pageviews per day**, roughly half of August's clean window (62/day) and slightly below July (37/day). That suggests August's "clean" window was still carrying spillover from the X post, and the X audience did not stick. The bright spot was **September 19--21, when a Facebook share sent 125 visitors to the homepage**, producing 347 pageviews, 7 quiz starts, 3 diagnostic signups and 2 practitioner inquiries in three days. Practitioner inquiries held at a healthy **11 for the month**, all on an organic cadence. **Google referrals fell by half (214 → 110)**, the one trend in this report worth acting on.

## Follow-up on August's recommendations

1. **Find the August 4 post and do it again.** No follow-up social activity is visible. t.co referrals fell to 13. The September Facebook share was organic, not a planned post.
2. **Ship the quiz routing experiment.** Not shipped. /diagnostics still has no quiz link. Fifth month recommended.
3. **Fix the quiz email screen.** Not shipped. Email capture dropped below half of completers.
4. **Design for mobile first.** Partly superseded. Outside social spikes the audience is two-thirds desktop again. Mobile still matters during spikes, which is when the most new visitors arrive.
5. **Give consumers their own door.** Not shipped. /contact was tried again.
6. **Repeat the LinkedIn post monthly.** No LinkedIn spike in September (5 referrals).
7. **Check the practitioner-onboarding pipeline.** /physicianagreement drew 1 view; JotForm referrals held at 5. No change visible from the analytics side.
8. **Keep the search engine running.** This is the item that went backwards: Google referrals halved. Blog still unlisted.

The site changes that did ship in September (Interventions heading, 12 paper links, the new Brain Health paper, quote moves) were client-requested content fixes, and /interventions engagement improved after them.

## Recommendations for October

1. **Pull Google Search Console for August and September.** Google halving is the most important number in this report. Search Console shows whether core rankings fell or whether August's branded searches simply faded. That decides whether anything needs fixing.
2. **Decide on the blog.** About 70 posts are written and unlisted. If Search Console shows rankings slipping, listing the blog (navigation link plus sitemap) is the single biggest search lever available.
3. **Ship the quiz routing experiment.** Add a quiz CTA to /diagnostics. The quiz converts and the traffic is there. The missing piece is the link.
4. **Fix the quiz email screen.** The same recommendation as August, now more pressing: fewer than half of completers leave an email.
5. **Plan the next share instead of waiting for one.** Two months in a row, a single social share (X in August, Facebook in September) was the month's biggest traffic event, and both converted. A planned monthly post with UTM-tagged links on X, Facebook or LinkedIn turns that into a repeatable channel.
6. **Keep calling on the investor signals.** Long Journey (12 days in September), SourceScrub, and links shared over Microsoft Teams all point to the site being passed around in investor circles.
