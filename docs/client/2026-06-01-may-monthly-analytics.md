### Engagement Signals

- **scroll_depth: 357 fires across 75 visitors** (~4.8 milestones each). Engaged visitors still go deep, even though fewer of them arrive.
- **CTA click rate: 15 / 1,047 = 1.4%**, down from April's 2.3%. This is the metric to fix. Lower traffic plus a softer homepage bounce (83%) means the CTA is being seen less and clicked less --- a homepage CTA design/copy test is the highest-leverage experiment for June.
- **nav_clicked 393 across 136 visitors** --- navigation is healthy; people explore the site once engaged.

## Recommendations for June

1. **Fix the top of the funnel, not the quiz.** Quiz completion is excellent (81%); the constraint is the ~1.5% start rate. Make the quiz entry point more prominent on the homepage and run a CTA copy/design test --- CTA click rate fell to 1.4% and is the single highest-leverage lever.
2. **Investigate /science's bounce regression (53% → 77%).** It was the best-engaging page at launch and is now average. Check whether April's imagery/layout edits changed how visitors reach it, and restore whatever made it sticky.
3. **Capitalize on Google's 86% bounce with content.** Organic search is the #2 channel but visitors arrive and leave. Pull Google Search Console, identify the winning queries, and point the (already-written) toxin-symptom blog posts at them. This is the clearest path to growing the top of the funnel.
4. **Lead with the practitioner channel.** Two months of 2:1 then 6:1 practitioner-to-consumer lead ratios is a strategy signal, not noise. Consider a dedicated practitioner landing page and practitioner-first messaging in June outreach.
5. **Follow up on longjourney.vc.** Two consecutive months of referral traffic from the same VC fund. Worth a direct conversation.
6. **Schedule an end-of-month push.** The final week of May averaged 25 pv/day --- the weakest window. A timed email or content drop around the 24th--28th could smooth the trough.
7. **Watch for first blog-driven traffic.** Posts added in April/May should begin indexing in June. If organic blog sessions appear, double down on the content plan; if not by July, reassess SEO approach.
